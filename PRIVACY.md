# Privacy Policy - Open in Freedium

_Last updated: 2026-06-16_

This Chrome extension ("the extension") is designed to do one thing: add a button
to Medium article pages that opens the current article on Freedium.

## Data we collect

**None.** The extension does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal
or usage data. Specifically, it does **not**:

- collect personally identifiable information (name, email, address, etc.);
- track your browsing history or activity;
- use cookies, analytics, or fingerprinting;
- send any data to the developer or any third party;
- use remote code - all logic ships inside the extension package.

## What the extension actually does

- Runs only on `medium.com` (and its subdomains), as declared in the manifest.
- When you click the "Read on Freedium" button, it opens a new browser tab at
  `https://freedium-mirror.cfd/<the current article URL>`. The current page URL is
  used solely to build that link locally in your browser; it is not logged or sent
  anywhere by the extension.

## Third-party services

Opening an article on Freedium navigates your browser to the Freedium website,
which is operated independently of this extension and has its own policies. This
extension is not affiliated with Medium or Freedium.

## Permissions

The extension requests access only to Medium pages, which is required to display
the button. It requests no other permissions.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the "Last updated" date above will be revised.
